The BÉTON project asked a single question: can a 6-meter dining table feel both monumental and warm?

We answered with three materials. A fair-faced concrete top for mass and silence. Solid oak legs for warmth and grain. Brushed steel connectors for precision. Each material does what it does best, and none pretends to be another.

The supervision platform logged every step — from concrete pour to final surface treatment — so the client watched the piece emerge from 8,000 kilometers away. Eleven weeks, start to delivery.
